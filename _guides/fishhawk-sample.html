Life on the Trails

More than 25 miles of trails wind under the oaks and connect FishHawk's villages, so you can walk, run or bike to parks, pools and Park Square without getting in the car.

When you're in the mood for an outing, there's usually something on the calendar: the Sunday market, seasonal festivals on the green and the Thanksgiving Turkey Trot.
