Living Here

Everything Within 10 Minutes

Brandon isn't one master-planned community. It's a big, established suburb with shopping, hospitals and highways all close by.

Shopping

Westfield Brandon & Brandon Blvd

The mall plus miles of stores and restaurants along Brandon Blvd (SR-60). Most errands are a short drive.

Healthcare

HCA Florida Brandon Hospital

A full-service hospital right in town, with doctors' offices and urgent care all around it.

Commute

Selmon, I-75 & I-4

The Selmon Expressway starts here, so downtown Tampa, South Tampa and MacDill are an easy run west.

Military

360LX to MacDill

HART's 360LX express runs weekdays from the J.C. Handly Park-n-Ride in Brandon to MacDill.

Homes

Mature Trees, Bigger Lots

Many neighborhoods were built in the '70s through the 2000s, so you get established trees and lots that newer areas don't have.

Value

More House for the Money

Prices are often lower than newer master-planned communities nearby, with no CDD on many homes.

The Stuff Nobody Tells You

What People Learn After They Move In

Check the roof age first

Lots of Brandon homes are 20 to 50 years old. Roof age drives your insurance price, and some insurers won't cover older roofs.

Ask about the pipes

Some older homes still have polybutylene or galvanized plumbing, which insurers ask about. Your inspector can tell you.

No HOA cuts both ways

No dues and no rules about your boat or RV, but also no one making the neighbor mow.

Drive SR-60 at 5 pm

Brandon Blvd backs up at rush hour. Living near your on-ramp matters more than miles.

Know your school zone

Brandon is split across several high school zones, so confirm the zone for each address.

Flood risk is street by street

Some low areas near creeks flooded after Hurricane Milton in 2024. Check the FEMA map and ask the seller.

Brandon FAQ

Common Questions

Is Brandon, FL a good place to live?

For many families, yes. It's central, with shopping, a hospital and the Selmon Expressway close by, and homes often cost less than newer master-planned communities. The trade-off is older homes, so insurance and inspections matter more.

How much are homes in Brandon, FL?

Over the last few months, most Brandon homes sold for between about $195,000 and $870,000, with a median of about $365,000. My monthly update above has the latest numbers.

Do Brandon homes have HOAs or CDDs?

Many older neighborhoods have no HOA or a low one, and CDDs are rare. Newer and gated communities have higher HOAs. Check the exact fees for any home you're considering.

How far is Brandon from MacDill AFB?

About 17 miles, or roughly 25 minutes without traffic, using the Selmon Expressway. Rush hour adds 15 to 30 minutes. See my Where to Live Near MacDill guide.

What schools serve Brandon?

Brandon is in Hillsborough County Public Schools and is split across several zones, including Bloomingdale, Armwood, Spoto and Brandon high schools. Confirm the zone for the exact address.

Is Brandon part of Tampa?

No. Brandon is an unincorporated community in Hillsborough County, about 11 miles east of downtown Tampa. Your local government is the county.

Does Brandon flood?

It depends on the street. Some low areas near creeks flooded after Hurricane Milton in 2024. Check the FEMA flood zone and ask for flood history, which Florida sellers now have to disclose.
