Which Part Fits You

FishHawk Isn't One Neighborhood

It's a collection of villages tied together by 25+ miles of trails. The right pick comes down to how you want your weekdays to feel.

Walk to everything

The Original Ranch & Park Square

Roughly $400K – $750K

The FishHawk people picture: mature oaks, established streets and the busiest community life. Park Square is the little town center with shops, restaurants and events. Pick it if you want to walk or bike to coffee.

Newer & easygoing

Starling

Roughly $350K – $520K

Newer homes, townhomes and single-family, plus its own club with a resort pool, fitness center and dog park. Pick it for newer construction and lower upkeep at the lower end of FishHawk pricing.

Fresh start

FishHawk Ranch West

Roughly $390K – $650K

The newest phase, with a modern agrarian look and The Lake House: pool, fitness center, fire pits and kayak access. Encore, a gated 55+ section, is here too.

Room to breathe

FishHawk Trails

Roughly $550K – $1.2M

Big-lot homes, typically half an acre to 2+ acres, with horse trail access and a separate HOA. Ask which Ranch amenities a given address can use. Pick it if the yard matters more than the walk to Park Square.

Price ranges are 2026 ballparks, not MLS data. Ask me for current numbers on any street.

Life in FishHawk

Trails, Oaks & Plenty to Do

More than 25 miles of trails wind under the oaks and connect the villages, so you can walk, run or bike to parks, pools and Park Square. And when you're in the mood for an outing, there's usually something on the calendar.

Local makers

FishHawk Ranch Market

Bakers, makers and small local businesses set up their tents at Cross Park. An easy Sunday stroll, and a good way to find your new favorite cookie.

Thanksgiving morning

The Turkey Trot

A FishHawk tradition. Thousands of runners and walkers (plenty in costume) earn their pie before the parade even starts.

On the green

Park Square Festivals

Seasonal festivals on the lawn at Park Square, with tents, music and food. Join in, or just enjoy the view from a café table.

Under the stars

Movie & Food Truck Nights

Bring a blanket, pick a truck and let the kids run around with friends while the movie starts.

Bragging rights

The Chili Cook-Off

Neighbors bring their best pot and everyone else gets to judge. Come hungry.

Treasure hunting

Community Garage Sale

One weekend, the whole neighborhood. Great for deals, and an easy way to get a feel for the streets before you buy.

Event dates change every year. Follow along on my monthly update for what's coming up.

The Lines to Add to Your Budget

Don't budget off the mortgage alone. Insurance and community fees are the lines that surprise people.

Don't use the seller's tax bill. Your assessment resets after you buy. Once you homestead, you get up to $50,000 off assessed value, and yearly increases are capped at 3%. File by March 1 with the Hillsborough County Property Appraiser.

The Stuff Nobody Tells You

What Most People Learn After They Move In

Your commute depends on the hour

Downtown Tampa is 22–25 miles away: about 35–45 minutes off-peak, 50–70 at morning rush. MacDill Air Force Base is about 30 miles, roughly 40 minutes with no traffic. Drive it at your real commute time before you make an offer.

The CDD hides on your tax bill

It's not a separate monthly bill, so it's easy to miss when comparing homes. Ask for the exact amount on any house you like.

Inland doesn't mean no flood risk

After Milton in 2024, the Alafia River hit its highest flooding in Lithia since 1933, mostly near the river. Check the flood zone and claims history for your address.

Your roof moves your insurance quote

Get quotes during your inspection period. A wind mitigation inspection runs about $75–$150 and can earn credits on newer homes.

A hurricane deductible isn't your regular deductible

On a $400,000 home, 2% means $8,000 out of pocket. Keep it in savings during storm season, June through November.

The amenities aren't all the same place

The Aquatic Club, Starling Club and The Lake House each have a different feel. Tour them, and ask which ones your address includes.

FishHawk Ranch FAQ

Questions I Get All the Time

How much is the CDD in FishHawk Ranch?

About $530 to $2,100 a year on the FY2026 chart, billed on your property tax bill. Older phases often run lower, and Starling and FishHawk Ranch West usually still carry bond payments.

What are the HOA fees in FishHawk Ranch?

The master HOA for single-family homes is about $100 to $300 a year. Townhomes and gated sections add an HOA of roughly $150 to $400+ a month, so check what it covers.

What schools serve FishHawk Ranch?

Newsome High, Randall and Barrington Middle, and Bevis, FishHawk Creek and Stowers Elementary are the schools most often zoned for FishHawk addresses, all A-rated for 2025-26. Always verify the exact address.

How far is FishHawk Ranch from downtown Tampa?

About 22 to 25 miles: roughly 35 to 45 minutes off-peak and 50 to 70 at morning rush on the free routes.

How far is FishHawk Ranch from MacDill Air Force Base?

About 30 miles, or roughly 40 minutes without traffic and longer at rush hour. Plenty of military families choose FishHawk for the A-rated schools and trails. If a shorter drive matters more, Riverview and Apollo Beach sit closer to the base, and I can help you compare. Run your numbers with my MacDill BAH calculator.

Does FishHawk Ranch flood?

Flood risk is address-specific. The Alafia River flooded near the river after Hurricane Milton in 2024, so check the flood zone and claims history for the exact home before you buy.
