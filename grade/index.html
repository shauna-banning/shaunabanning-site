    What is schema?

    It’s a name tag for your website, written for machines. People see your logo and work out that you’re a bakery in Brandon. Schema says it outright, in a small block of hidden code.

    Google calls it “explicit clues” about what a page means. It should match what visitors can see. It helps you be understood, but it doesn’t guarantee a ranking, and Google says its AI answers have no special requirements.

    {
  "@type": "Bakery",
  "name": "Sample Corner Bakery",
  "telephone": "(813) 555-0100",
  "address": "100 Main St, Brandon, FL",
  "areaServed": "Brandon and Valrico"
}
