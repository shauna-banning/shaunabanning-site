BAH figures are 2026 Defense Travel Management Office rates for the Tampa military housing area (MacDill AFB), effective Jan 1, 2026, and change each January; confirm yours with the official DoD BAH calculator. Area figures are fall 2026 medians from MLS data and rental sites (RentCafe, Trulia, Rentometer), not current listings; FishHawk uses the Stellar MLS 12-month median sale price. HOA/CDD amounts vary by community. VA funding fee uses current VA tiers and is added to the loan. Sale estimate assumes 7% selling costs and 2% buyer closing costs; maintenance and home value growth use the rates you enter; rent assumes 3% yearly increases. Picking a Suburb

Which Suburb Fits Your Commute?

Riverview and Apollo Beach sit closest to the base. Brandon and Valrico are a middle ground. FishHawk is about 30 miles out, roughly 40 minutes without traffic, and many families choose it for the A-rated schools and trails.
