The Stuff Nobody Tells You

What People Learn After They Move In

Insurance first, house second

On the water, insurance can change the whole budget. Get homeowners and flood quotes before you write an offer.

Check the flood and evacuation zone

Look up both for the exact address, and ask the seller for any flood claims history (Florida now requires the disclosure).

Know your bridge heights

If you boat, check the canal route and any fixed-bridge clearances for your boat.

Seawalls are a big-ticket item

Ask the age and condition of the seawall, and who's responsible for repairs.

Know the evacuation zone

Coastal communities evacuate first in a storm. Know your zone and route before hurricane season.

Not every home is on the water

Prices vary widely between waterfront, water-view and interior homes. Compare like with like.

MiraBay FAQ

Common Questions

Does MiraBay have a CDD?

Yes. The CDD assessment is billed on the property tax bill and helps fund the community amenities. The amount varies by lot.

Is MiraBay in a flood zone?

Many homes are, since it's a coastal, canal-front community. Check the FEMA flood zone and evacuation zone for the exact address and get a flood insurance quote early.

What schools serve MiraBay?

Listings show Apollo Beach K-8 (A-rated in 2026) and Lennard High (B). Confirm zoning with the district for the exact address.

Does MiraBay have boat access to Tampa Bay?

Many canal-front homes have Tampa Bay access. Check the specific route and any bridge clearances for your boat.

What amenities does MiraBay have?

Heated pools (one with a slide), a clubhouse with food and drinks, a fitness center, tennis, pickleball, basketball and a kayak launch.

How far is MiraBay from MacDill AFB?

About 25 miles, or roughly 35 to 55 minutes at weekday morning rush. See my Where to Live Near MacDill guide.
