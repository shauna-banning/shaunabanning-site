Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy covers shaunabanning.com, run by Shauna Banning, REALTOR® with Coldwell Banker Realty, 213 W Bloomingdale Ave, Brandon, FL 33511.

What I collect

  • What you type into a form. The home value form asks for your email and property address, and the follow-up page can ask for your phone number and details about your home. Newsletter sign-ups ask for your name and email.
  • Basic visit data. Google Analytics records pages viewed, device type, general location and when a form is sent. It uses cookies and does not tell me who you are.
  • Answers to the free self-checks (like the Google Profile Check) stay in your browser and in the link you copy. They are not sent to me.

How I use it

  • To prepare the report or information you asked for and to answer your questions.
  • To send my monthly market update if you signed up. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To call or text you about your request, only if you gave your number and agreed. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Message and data rates may apply.
  • To see which pages are useful so I can improve the site.

Who sees it

I do not sell or rent your information. It is stored with the services that run this site: Google (Sheets, Apps Script and Analytics), Flodesk (email) and GitHub Pages (hosting). My brokerage, Coldwell Banker Realty, may see it if your request becomes a real estate transaction. I share it with anyone else only if you ask me to or the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Ask me what I have about you, or ask me to correct or delete it, by emailing shaunasellstampa@gmail.com.
  • Unsubscribe from any email using the link at the bottom.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on.

Children

This site is not meant for anyone under 18, and I do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
