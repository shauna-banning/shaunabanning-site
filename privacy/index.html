Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information shaunabanning.com collects and how it’s used. This site is run by Shauna Banning, REALTOR® with Coldwell Banker Realty, 213 W Bloomingdale Ave, Brandon, FL 33511.

Information you give me

When you fill out a form on this site (a home value request, a monthly market update sign-up, or a free download), I collect what you enter, such as your name, email, phone number and property address. I use it only to send what you asked for, to follow up about your real estate questions, and to send updates you signed up for. I never sell your information.

Email

Email sign-ups are managed through Flodesk. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time.

Website analytics

To understand how visitors use this site and improve it, I use:

  • Google Analytics, which uses cookies to measure visits, pages viewed and how people found the site.
  • Microsoft Clarity, which records how visitors interact with pages (clicks, scrolling and mouse movement) through heat maps and session replays. Clarity masks text you type into forms.

These tools see visits in general, not who you are. You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings, or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Learn more in Google’s privacy policy and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Text messages

If you give me your phone number, I may call or text you about your request. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.
