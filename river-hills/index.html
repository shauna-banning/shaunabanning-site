The Stuff Nobody Tells You

What People Learn After They Move In

Know what the HOA covers

Some sections include a club social membership and some don't. Ask for the HOA's budget and what's included before you compare homes.

Golf is optional

You don't have to golf to live here, and many residents don't. Full golf membership is a separate cost.

Roof age drives insurance

Many homes date from the 1990s–2000s. A newer roof can save thousands a year on insurance.

Ask about the club's plans

Clubs change hands and update their plans. Ask the HOA about the club's current status and any planned assessments.

Lot sizes vary a lot

Some streets have much bigger lots than others. If yard space matters, compare lot sizes, not just square footage.

Check for an HOA budget and reserves

In an established community, the HOA's reserves tell you whether big projects are covered or could mean a special assessment.

River Hills FAQ

Common Questions

What are the HOA fees in River Hills?

Recent listings show roughly $290 to $680 a quarter depending on section, and some include a social membership to the country club. Confirm the exact fee and what it covers.

Do you have to join the River Hills Country Club?

Full golf membership is separate from the HOA and optional. Many listings say HOA dues include social access, so check what's included for the specific home.

What schools serve River Hills?

Listings commonly show Lithia Springs Elementary, Randall Middle and Newsome High, all A-rated by the state for 2026. Always confirm zoning for the exact address with the district.

Is there a CDD in River Hills?

Many established sections don't have a CDD, but check the property tax bill for the specific home to be sure.

How old are the homes in River Hills?

Most homes were built from the late 1980s through the 2000s, so roof age, AC age and updates matter when comparing homes.

How far is River Hills from MacDill AFB?

About 25 miles, or roughly 40 to 60 minutes at weekday morning rush. See my Where to Live Near MacDill guide.
