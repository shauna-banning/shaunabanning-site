Living Here

New Homes, Pools & Young Families

Most of Riverview has been built since 2000, so a lot of it is master-planned communities with amenity centers, sidewalks and ponds.

Amenities

Community Pools & Clubhouses

Many neighborhoods have their own resort-style pool, playgrounds and a fitness center.

Healthcare

Two Hospitals

AdventHealth Riverview and St. Joseph's Hospital-South are both right in Riverview.

Errands

Big Bend & US-301

Groceries, Target, restaurants and coffee line Big Bend Road and US-301.

Outside

Alafia River

Kayak the Alafia, or head to Alafia River State Park for trails and mountain biking.

Commute

I-75 & the Selmon

I-75 runs right through, and US-301 connects north to the Selmon for Tampa and MacDill.

New Builds

New Construction

Builders are still active here, so you can compare new homes with lightly used resales.

What It Really Costs

HOA, CDD & Insurance

Your monthly cost is more than the mortgage. Here's everything else to plan for in Riverview.

CostRough rangeWhat to know

HOA~$10 – $250 / moMost newer neighborhoods have one. Covers common areas and the amenity center.

The Stuff Nobody Tells You

What People Learn After They Move In

The CDD hides on your tax bill

It isn't a separate monthly bill, so it's easy to miss. Ask for the exact amount on every home.

Compare total monthly cost

Two similar homes can differ by hundreds a month once you add the CDD, HOA and insurance.

Drive Big Bend at rush hour

Big Bend, US-301 and Boyette back up at peak times. Drive your real commute before you make an offer.

New build vs. resale

Builder incentives can look great, but a 2-year-old resale often comes with blinds, a fence and a lower price. Compare both.

School zones changed recently

Sumner High opened in 2023 and redrew some zones. Confirm the school for each address.

Flood risk is street by street

Some areas near the Alafia River flooded after Hurricane Milton in 2024. Check the FEMA map and ask the seller.

Riverview FAQ

Common Questions

Is Riverview, FL a good place to live?

For many young families, yes. It has newer homes, community pools, two hospitals and good access to I-75. The trade-offs are traffic on Big Bend and US-301, and CDD fees in most newer neighborhoods.

How much are homes in Riverview?

Over the last few months, most Riverview homes sold for between about $215,000 and $740,000, with a median of about $375,000. My monthly update above has the latest numbers.

Do Riverview homes have CDD fees?

Most newer communities do, often about $1,000 to $3,000 a year, billed on the property tax bill. Older neighborhoods usually don't.

How far is Riverview from MacDill AFB?

About 18 miles, or roughly 26 minutes without traffic. Rush hour adds 15 to 30 minutes. See my Where to Live Near MacDill guide.

What schools serve Riverview?

Riverview is in Hillsborough County Public Schools. High schools include Riverview (A in 2026), Spoto, Sumner and East Bay, depending on the address.

Is Riverview part of Tampa?

No. Riverview is an unincorporated community in Hillsborough County, about 13 miles southeast of downtown Tampa.

Does Riverview flood?

It depends on the street. Some areas near the Alafia River flooded after Hurricane Milton in 2024. Check the FEMA flood zone and ask for flood history, which Florida sellers now have to disclose.
