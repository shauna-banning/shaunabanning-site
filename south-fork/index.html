Living Here

South Fork is a collection of neighborhoods built around a shared amenity center, with sidewalks, ponds and parks throughout.

Amenity center

The Stuff Nobody Tells You

What People Learn After They Move In

There's more than one "South Fork"

South Fork is made up of several subdivisions, and Southfork Lakes is a separate community with its own HOA. Confirm exactly which association a home belongs to.

The CDD hides on your tax bill

It's not a separate monthly bill, so it's easy to miss. Ask for the exact amount on any home you like.

Drive it at rush hour

US-301, Big Bend and I-75 back up at peak times. Drive your real commute before you make an offer.

Get insurance quotes early

Roof age moves your premium more than almost anything. Quote it during your inspection period, not after closing.

Pets have rules

South Fork allows dogs and cats, but some sections have breed, size or number limits. Check before you fall for a house.

Compare total monthly cost

Two similar homes can differ by hundreds a month once you add the CDD, HOA and insurance. Compare the full payment, not the price.

South Fork FAQ

Common Questions

How much is the CDD in South Fork?

Recent listings show roughly $2,000 to $2,700 a year, billed on the property tax bill. It varies by section and lot, so check the exact amount for any home you're considering.

What are the HOA fees in South Fork?

Most listings show low monthly dues, roughly $10 to $45 a month, though some sub-areas run higher. Confirm the exact association and fee for the home.

What schools serve South Fork?

Listings show Summerfield Crossings Elementary and Eisenhower Middle. High school is Sumner or East Bay depending on the section, since Sumner opened in 2023 and took over part of East Bay's zone. Confirm with the district for the exact address.

How far is South Fork from MacDill AFB?

About 22 to 25 miles, or roughly 35 to 55 minutes at weekday morning rush depending on your route. See my Where to Live Near MacDill guide.

How much are homes in South Fork?

In November 2026, listings ranged from about $270,000 to $825,000, from 3-bed homes around 1,600 sq ft to 6-bed homes near 8,000 sq ft. My monthly update has the latest sold prices.

What amenities does South Fork have?

A clubhouse, pool and spa, fitness center, tennis, pickleball and basketball courts, a dog park, playgrounds, a community park and walking trails.

Does South Fork flood?

Flood risk depends on the address. Check the FEMA flood zone and ask the seller for flood history, which Florida sellers now have to disclose.

Can I have pets in South Fork?

Yes, dogs and cats are allowed, though some sections have breed, size or number limits. Check the rules for the specific association.
