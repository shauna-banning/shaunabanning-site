The Stuff Nobody Tells You

What People Learn After They Move In

The CDD is the big number

HOA dues are low, but the CDD often runs $3,000 or more a year on the tax bill. Compare total monthly cost, not just the HOA.

Some villages have two associations

Ask for every association fee on the home, not just the master HOA.

It's farther east than it looks

Triple Creek sits east of US-301, so drives to Tampa and MacDill run longer than the rest of Riverview. Drive it at rush hour.

Construction traffic is temporary

Building is still going on in parts of the community. Visit on a weekday to see what daily life is like right now.

School is close, so check the walk zone

Dawson Elementary sits inside Triple Creek. Some homes are close enough to walk, which matters for bus service. Ask the school.

New build or resale?

New homes come with builder warranties but often bigger CDDs. Resale homes may have landscaping and window treatments done. Price out both.

Triple Creek FAQ

Common Questions

How much is the CDD in Triple Creek?

Recent listings show CDD assessments often around $3,000 or more a year, billed on the property tax bill. It varies by village and lot.

What schools serve Triple Creek?

Listings most often show Warren Hope Dawson Elementary (inside the community), Barrington Middle and Sumner High. Always confirm zoning for the exact address with the district.

What amenities does Triple Creek have?

Multiple amenity centers with pools, a splash pad, a fitness center, sports fields, tennis and basketball courts, a dog park, parks and playgrounds.

How far is Triple Creek from MacDill AFB?

Roughly 25 to 28 miles, or about 40 to 60 minutes at weekday morning rush. See my Where to Live Near MacDill guide.

Are there new homes in Triple Creek?

Yes, builders are still active in some villages, so you'll find both new construction and resale homes.

Does Triple Creek flood?

Flood risk depends on the address. Check the FEMA flood zone and the seller's flood disclosure for the specific home.
