Living Here

Room to Breathe, Close to Brandon

Valrico feels more spread out than Brandon: bigger lots, more trees and a mix of established neighborhoods, golf communities and a little acreage.

Space

Bigger Lots

Many homes sit on larger lots than you'll find in newer master-planned communities, and some areas allow boats and RVs.

Schools

A-Rated Options

Bloomingdale High, Durant High, Mulrennan Middle and several elementary schools earned A's in 2026.

Golf

River Hills & Buckhorn

Two golf communities with wooded lots and bigger homes.

Errands

Brandon Next Door

Westfield Brandon, the hospital and Brandon Blvd shopping are 10 to 15 minutes away.

Outside

Alafia River

The Alafia River and Alafia River State Park are close for kayaking, mountain biking and trails.

Commute

SR-60 & the Selmon

Head west on SR-60 or Bloomingdale to the Selmon for Tampa and MacDill.

The Stuff Nobody Tells You

What People Learn After They Move In

Septic is normal here

Lots of larger-lot homes are on septic. Get it inspected and pumped records before closing.

Check for a well

Some homes have a private well. Test the water and ask how old the pump is.

Zoning decides the schools

Valrico has strong schools, but zones split by street. Confirm the zone for each address.

Drive Bloomingdale & SR-60 at rush hour

The roads out to the Selmon and I-75 slow down a lot at peak times.

Roof age moves insurance

Many homes are from the '80s to 2000s. Insurers care most about the roof's age.

Flood risk is street by street

Low areas near the Alafia River and creeks flooded after Hurricane Milton in 2024. Check the FEMA map and ask the seller.

Valrico FAQ

Common Questions

Is Valrico, FL a good place to live?

For families who want space and strong schools, yes. Several Valrico-area schools earned A's in 2026, and lots are bigger than in newer communities. The trade-off is a longer drive to Tampa than from Brandon.

How much are homes in Valrico?

Over the last few months, most Valrico homes sold for between about $220,000 and $735,000, with a median of about $451,000. My monthly update above has the latest numbers.

What's the difference between Valrico and Brandon?

Valrico is just east of Brandon. It's quieter, with bigger lots and more A-rated schools, while Brandon has more shopping and a shorter commute.

Do Valrico homes have CDDs?

Most don't, though some newer neighborhoods do. HOAs range from none on acreage homes to higher dues in golf communities.

How far is Valrico from MacDill AFB?

About 20 miles, or roughly 30 minutes without traffic, using the Selmon Expressway. Rush hour adds 15 to 30 minutes. See my Where to Live Near MacDill guide.

What schools serve Valrico?

Bloomingdale and Durant high schools serve most of Valrico, and some areas go to Newsome. All three earned A's in 2026. Confirm the zone for the exact address.

Does Valrico flood?

It depends on the street. Some low areas near the Alafia River and creeks flooded after Hurricane Milton in 2024. Check the FEMA flood zone and ask for flood history, which Florida sellers now have to disclose.
