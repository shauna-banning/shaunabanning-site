The Stuff Nobody Tells You

What People Learn After They Move In

Coastal means flood homework

Even newer homes built to higher standards can sit in a flood or evacuation zone. Check both for the exact lot and get a flood quote.

Know what's included

Some amenities may require an optional club membership. Ask exactly what the HOA and CDD cover.

The CDD varies by village

Two similar homes can carry very different CDD amounts. Compare total monthly cost.

US-41 and I-75 at rush hour

Most drives north run through US-41 or I-75. Drive your real commute before you commit.

Ask which village

Each village has its own CDD amount, and some have their own HOA. Know which village a home is in before comparing prices.

Builders compete with resale

New-home incentives can make resale homes look pricey. Compare total cost, including builder closing-cost credits.

Waterset FAQ

Common Questions

How much is the CDD in Waterset?

Community and builder materials show roughly $1,970 to $3,470 a year, billed on the property tax bill, varying by village and lot.

What schools serve Waterset?

Listings most often show Doby Elementary, Eisenhower Middle and East Bay High. Waterset Charter School (K–8) is also on site. Confirm zoning with the district for the exact address.

Is Waterset in a flood zone?

It depends on the lot. Waterset is in coastal south Hillsborough, so check the FEMA flood zone and evacuation zone for the specific address.

How far is Waterset from MacDill AFB?

About 25 miles, or roughly 35 to 55 minutes at weekday morning rush. See my Where to Live Near MacDill guide.

What amenities does Waterset have?

Clubhouse and pools, a fitness center, a splash pad, a dog park, trails, playgrounds and soccer, tennis and basketball courts.

Are there new homes in Waterset?

Yes, builders are still active, so you'll find new construction and resale homes side by side.
