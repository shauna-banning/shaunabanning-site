The Stuff Nobody Tells You

What PCS Families Learn After They Arrive

Drive it at your real report time

A drive that's 25 minutes on Saturday can be 50 on a Tuesday at 6:45am. Before you sign anything, check your address in a map app with an "arrive by" time, or drive it yourself during house hunting.

Know your gate

The Dale Mabry Gate is open 24/7. The Bayshore Gate keeps shorter hours, and other gates open only at certain times. Check current gate hours, because they change.

The Selmon has reversible express lanes

From Brandon and Riverview, the Selmon Expressway is the fastest route west for many people. It's a toll road, and its express lanes switch direction by time of day. Prefer not to drive? HART's 360LX commuter bus runs weekdays from Brandon's J.C. Handly Park-n-Ride to MacDill. Check goHART.org for the current schedule.

Inland doesn't mean no flood risk

Coastal South Tampa and Apollo Beach face storm surge. Inland, homes near the Alafia River flooded after Hurricane Milton in 2024. Check the FEMA flood map for any address you're serious about.

Florida now requires flood disclosures

Sellers must disclose known flood damage and flood insurance claims, and landlords must disclose flood history on leases of a year or longer. Ask for these forms early, whether you're buying or renting.

Get insurance quotes before you fall in love

Roof age, flood zone and distance from the water can swing a homeowners policy by thousands a year. Quote it during your inspection period, not after closing.

Call the School Liaison as soon as you have orders

The MacDill School Liaison Program and the Hillsborough County Public Schools military liaison can explain zoning, school choice and transferring records. Your school depends on your exact address, so check before you commit to a home.

CDD fees hide on the tax bill

Many newer communities in Riverview, Apollo Beach and FishHawk have a CDD assessment billed on your property taxes. Ask for the exact amount on every home you compare.

MacDill PCS FAQ

Common Questions

What is the best area to live near MacDill AFB?

It depends on what matters most. South Tampa has the shortest drive but the highest prices and the most flood exposure. Brandon and Riverview offer the most house for your BAH with a 30 to 55 minute rush-hour drive. FishHawk Ranch has A-rated schools and trails but the longest commute. Apollo Beach offers water access with coastal flood and insurance homework.

How long is the commute to MacDill from Brandon or Riverview?

Roughly 30 to 55 minutes at weekday morning rush, depending on where you live and whether you take the Selmon Expressway (a toll road). Check your exact address in a map app with an "arrive by" time before you commit.

Should I live on base or off base?

On base means almost no commute, but your BAH goes to the housing provider and there can be a waitlist. Off base, you choose your schools and space and keep any BAH you don't spend. Contact the MacDill housing office as soon as you have orders to learn current wait times.

What schools do MacDill families use?

Most MacDill children attend Hillsborough County Public Schools. Families on base are zoned for Tinker K-8, a Purple Star school on MacDill. Off base, your school depends on your exact address. The MacDill School Liaison Program can help as soon as you have orders.

Do I need flood insurance near MacDill?

Many homes near the base and along the bay are in flood zones, and inland homes near rivers flooded after Hurricane Milton in 2024. Check the FEMA flood zone and flood history for the exact address, and get insurance quotes before your inspection period ends.

Should I rent or buy on a 3-year tour?

On a 2 to 3 year tour, renting often comes out ahead once you count closing costs, selling costs and the VA funding fee. Buying tends to win on longer stays or if you plan to retire in Tampa. Run your own numbers with my MacDill BAH calculator.
